Section 01

The brand at a glance.

RUKCON11 is the eleventh edition of an annual, invitation-only convention for top-performing entrepreneurs, operators, and executives. The "ALL IN" tag signals total commitment — chips on the table, no hedge.

The visual identity is built around three forces:

  • GOLD — primary. Earned, not flashy. Aged-coin gold, never neon.
  • TEAL — supporting. The brushstroke. Sun-bleached, slightly Caribbean. Lets the wives' presence cut through the masculine register.
  • GRIT — texture, brushstroke marks, condensed type, almost-square corners. We are not a SaaS dashboard.

Section 08 · Voice

Direct. Earned. A little salty.

RUKCON11 talks to people who run things. Don't explain the obvious. Don't hedge. The reader has a P&L and a calendar that's already on fire — respect both.

You're not here to network.

You're here because the people you needed to meet four years ago are sitting two seats over.

Three days. No panels.

No badges that say "VP of Synergy." Bring the person you'd call from a foxhole.

DO

  • "You're at the table."
  • "Eleven years. One room."
  • "Bring the person you'd call from a foxhole."
  • Em-dashes. Periods after display lines.
  • "Couples" or "partners in the room."

DON'T

  • "Join us for an unforgettable journey of growth."
  • "Unlock your full potential, surrounded by like-minded leaders."
  • "Bring your spouse along!"
  • Synergize. Leverage. Unlock.
  • Emoji. Exclamation marks. Hedge words.

Section 09 · Iconography & caveats

Outlined. 1.5px. Sparingly used.

Iconography

Outlined, 1.5px stroke, square caps, 24×24 base. Library: Lucide. Icons inherit --fg by default; --accent (gold) for emphasis; never paint icons teal except for nav-active states on partner-facing surfaces.

  • Use for: agenda items, room markers, dietary flags.
  • Don't decorate headlines.
  • Don't replace words a senior reader can read.

Emoji & unicode

No emoji in product UI. Acceptable separators: · (mid-dot), — (em-dash). Never bullet •.

Caveats — please review

  1. No source codebase or Figma provided — system is logo-derived.
  2. Brand font confirmed: Montserrat. "ALL IN" hand-lettering remains custom art — never re-typeset.
  3. Iconography defaulted to Lucide. Confirm or send a set.
  4. No UI kit or slide template generated — no product surface was provided. Send one and we'll build against real source.
  5. Voice rules are speculative — derived from the "ALL IN" tagline. Real samples will sharpen this fast.
